# Brand-kit system

This folder is the delivery root for every Tangison group-business kit. Each kit uses the same numbered structure so files can be found without a separate handover.

```text
00_Overview/       Kit index, legal particulars, contact source and missing list
01_Identity/       Approved logos and usage notes
02_Colour/         Swatches and colour specifications
03_Type/           Licensed font files and type specimen
04_Print/           Business card, ID card, certificate and stationery
05_Documents/      Quotation, receipt, NDA and agreement templates
06_Social/         Social-post source files, exports and posting notes
07_Profile/        Platform avatar and banner exports
08_Digital/        Email signature and other digital office assets
```

Do not recreate a logo or alter locked identity artwork. Keep editable source files, print-ready PDFs and screen exports together in the appropriate numbered folder. A kit may not claim legal status, registration, accreditation, a service, a person or a contact detail that has not been confirmed for that business.

## Social post families

The following system applies to every business. A post uses one family only; it does not combine competing headline, quote and checklist treatments.

| Family | Layout rule | Headline rule | Mark placement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Photo and headline | One image occupies the full field; place the type in a single calm edge band or clear area of the image. | One sentence, maximum two lines. Use the display face at approximately 10 to 12 percent of canvas width. | Small approved lockup in the lower right, inset 5 percent from each edge. |
| Big quote | Plain brand ground with one quotation as the sole focal point. | Maximum 18 words, set at approximately 11 to 14 percent of canvas width. Attribute only if verified. | Small approved lockup in the lower right, inset 5 percent. |
| Stat | One verified figure with its source and date visible in the layout. | The figure is the headline. Supporting text is one short line. | Small approved lockup in the lower right, inset 5 percent. |
| Checklist | Three to five short, parallel items with generous line spacing. | A practical imperative or question, maximum two lines. | Small approved lockup in the lower right, inset 5 percent. |
| Announcement | One confirmed public fact, date and next step. | Maximum two lines. Never use a future date or approval claim unless formally confirmed. | Small approved lockup in the lower right, inset 5 percent. |

Use a 1080 by 1350 primary social canvas unless a platform requires another documented ratio. Keep text and marks inside a 10 percent safe area. Exports must remain legible at mobile size. Captions carry links, sources and necessary qualifications; the graphic remains concise.
