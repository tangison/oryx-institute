# Oryx Institute scroll plan

This plan follows `BRIEF.md`. It is the first row in the local Scroll Craft registry, so the fingerprint gate has no existing build to compare against.

## Grammar: Grounded field ledger

A dimensional observation opens the homepage, then the site reads as a sequence of editorial working papers. The grammar is built for an institute that earns trust through public, practical evidence.

- **Navigation:** existing accessible masthead, menu and language switch remain the orientation system. The homepage pathway selector is content, never a replacement navigation layer.
- **Hero:** layered existing Oryx field image, direct claim, real actions. Text sits on its own local readable ground.
- **Sequence:** observation → method → proof → pathway choice → evidence → office invitation.
- **Close:** the existing direct office/application actions resolve the journey and remain usable without motion.
- **Explicit bans:** no section number UI, progress counter, generic card grid, fake surface, full-frame opaque overlay, fake data, autoplay audio/video, text baked into an image, or decorative scroll prompt.

### Why the other established grammars lost

- **Filmic one-shot:** too linear and too promotional for an evidence-led institute. It also conflicts with the user’s selected editorial resolution.
- **Chaptered editorial:** closest fit, but its hard no-fixed-navigation rule would break the established accessible site navigation and language controls.
- **Live surface:** Oryx is not selling a software interface.
- **Continuous world:** the user asked for an opening world and editorial chapters, not a single uninterrupted spatial journey. The subject does not need worldflight.
- **Typographic poster:** the institute has useful real imagery and evidence, so type alone would erase practical texture.
- **Gallery, catalogue and service-menu grammars:** they flatten the journey into browsing and diminish the held proof-of-practice peak.

## Feeling curve, before devices

| Act | Intended feeling | What causes it | Span |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arrival | Grounded curiosity | Quiet field image and direct claim | Standard hero |
| Method | Trust | Named ways of working, no abstract promise | Deliberate editorial chapter |
| Proof, peak | Conviction | Held practical-work image and precise copy | Largest visual hold |
| Field note | Agency | An optional, working pathway choice that becomes a real next step | Short interactive chapter |
| Evidence | Reassurance | Live offers, research and dated public material | Editorial chapters |
| Close | Readiness | One truthful action to apply or write | Resolved close |

The proof act is the only peak. The quieter field-note act immediately after it restores agency before the evidence-led close.

## Score table

| Beat | Device family | Why this device |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival | layered parallax / scrub | Existing field image gains depth while retaining a readable, non-opaque text ground. |
| Method | pinned crossfade | The three named ways of working arrive one at a time, so each can be read. |
| Proof, peak | pinned media hold | The practical-work image gets the largest uninterrupted space and no invented metric competes with it. |
| Field note | bespoke interactive choice | Visitors can set a real interest and receive a matching real link; this is the signature move. |
| Evidence | flow, reveal and material dividers | Offers, research and register rows need inspectable reading rhythm rather than spectacle. |
| Close | flow with resolved action | The office invitation remains stable, direct and accessible. |

The homepage uses six non-adjacent device families: parallax, pin/crossfade, pin/media hold, bespoke interaction, flow/reveal, and material editorial divider. The existing motion layer already gives static, no-JavaScript and reduced-motion fallbacks. This work does not modify the shared Scroll Craft engine.

## Technical constraints

- Existing `public/brand/` identity files remain byte-for-byte untouched.
- Existing internal imagery only. No generated assets and no embeds or third-party social scripts.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` keeps content in normal flow, removes pin/parallax travel and leaves all controls operable.
- The Field notes trace persists only in `sessionStorage` for a browser session. It sends no data and provides no analytics or cookies.
- Mobile is a separate composition: the sticky field note becomes normal-flow content and all tap targets stay at least 44px.
- There is no full ffmpeg binary in the sandbox. Asset generation and scrub-video encoding are therefore intentionally out of scope. Existing image-based motion and browser checks remain eligible.
