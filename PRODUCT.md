# PRODUCT.md - Oryx Institute

> One-line product definition: **We are building a pre-launch marketing and learner-interest website for Oryx Institute, a multidisciplinary vocational education and training institution being established in Windhoek, Namibia, so that prospective learners, employers, WIL partners, corporate training clients, research and advisory clients, funding partners, and the Namibian public can understand the institution, register interest by programme, and submit partnership enquiries, supporting the institution's public launch and intake conversion.**

---

## 1. Organisation

**Oryx Institute** is a multidisciplinary vocational education and training institution being established in Windhoek, Namibia. It is pre-launch. It is being developed by founder **Tangi Iigonda**. The institution intends to deliver classroom-based training, recognition of prior learning (RPL), work-integrated learning (WIL), applied research, workforce studies, organisational training, and advisory services across multiple planned schools.

The institution is in active establishment. The following facts are NOT verified and must NOT be presented as established: legal registration number, accreditation status, programme approval status, campus address, partner organisations, learner numbers, qualification catalogue, fee schedule, intake dates, staff complement.

## 2. Audience

Primary audiences, in priority order:

1. **Prospective learners** in Windhoek and across Namibia, including school leavers, working adults seeking formal qualifications, and people with workplace experience seeking recognition through RPL.
2. **Employers** in Namibian industry, hospitality, retail, administration, safety, and digital sectors seeking trained staff or workplace learning partnerships.
3. **WIL placement partners** who can host learners in real workplaces for supervised practice.
4. **Corporate training clients** seeking bespoke organisational training.
5. **Research and advisory clients** including government departments, NGOs, and industry associations needing workforce studies, applied research, or advisory services.
6. **Funding and institutional partners** including donors, development finance institutions, public skills funds, and academic partners.
7. **Investors and donors** evaluating the institution during its establishment phase.
8. **The Namibian public** seeking to understand what is being built.

Secondary audiences: media, regulators, peer institutions, and academic researchers.

## 3. Offer

Oryx Institute is being developed to offer:

- Classroom-based vocational training across multiple schools.
- Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) assessment for experienced workers seeking formal qualifications.
- Work-integrated learning (WIL) placements with employer partners.
- Applied research and workforce studies.
- Organisational training and advisory services.

Planned schools (subject to approval, not yet registered qualifications):

- School of Safety
- School of Administration and Commerce
- School of Hospitality and Tourism
- School of Information and Digital Skills
- Future Schools (announced as the institution grows)

The website itself offers, in pre-launch mode:

- An honest, beautiful presentation of what is being built.
- A programme browser showing planned programmes with status: "Planned", "Subject to approval", or "Register your interest".
- A learning-pathways explainer covering classroom learning, RPL, WIL, assessment, and progression.
- A campus concept presentation clearly labelled as architectural concept, not completed facilities.
- A research and advisory introduction.
- An establishment updates feed for verified institutional updates.
- A primary Register Interest form.
- Separate enquiry paths for prospective learners, employers, WIL partners, corporate training clients, research and advisory clients, and funding and institutional partners.
- An institutional mailing list.
- A founder page with minimal copy about Tangi Iigonda.

## 4. Positioning

Oryx Institute is positioned as **a quietly prestigious Namibian institution** rooted in the Namibian landscape, work, and dignity. It is not positioned as a technology startup, a school template, a corporate training mill, or an international franchise.

The institution differentiates on:

- **Place.** Namibian, not international. Rooted in Windhoek and the wider Namibian landscape.
- **Discipline.** Academic in character, vocational in practice.
- **Restraint.** Quiet, minimal, architectural, warm. Not loud, not generic, not crowded.
- **Honesty.** Pre-launch status stated clearly. No fabricated accreditation, no fabricated partners, no fabricated metrics.
- **Multiple pathways.** Classroom learning plus RPL plus WIL plus research and advisory, not classroom-only.
- **Editorial photography.** Original commissioned imagery of Namibian landscapes, learning objects, and people, not stock photography.

## 5. Primary and secondary conversions

**Primary conversion: Register Interest.**

The Register Interest form captures:

- Name
- Email
- Telephone or WhatsApp number
- Region or town
- Programme of interest
- Current education level
- Employment status
- Interest in RPL
- Preferred study schedule
- Consent to receive updates

**Secondary conversions:**

- Joining the institutional mailing list (lighter form: name, email, consent).
- Submitting an employer enquiry.
- Submitting a WIL placement partner enquiry.
- Submitting a corporate training enquiry.
- Submitting a research and advisory enquiry.
- Submitting a funding or institutional partnership enquiry.
- Downloading approved prospectus material when available.

Every call to action must lead to a real destination. No dead ends. No "coming soon" pages that contain only the words "coming soon".

- **Brand purpose:** Give Namibians practical, recognised skills through vocational education rooted in the Namibian landscape, work, and dignity.
- **Brand promise:** Knowledge shaped by the Namibian landscape.

## 7. Design direction

(See BRAND.md and BUILD_PLAN.md for the complete system.)

- **Visual idea:** Knowledge shaped by the Namibian landscape. Restrained visual vocabulary: oryx, dunes, stone, books, architecture, paper, shadows, ordinary learning objects.
- **Layout:** Large areas of negative space. Fine rules. Disciplined grids. Deliberate asymmetry.
- **Photography:** Warm editorial realism. Cream and sandstone colour cast. Muted maroon accents. Soft sepia undertone. Strong but natural directional sunlight. Minimal compositions. One main subject per image. No text. No logos. No fantasy architecture. No visibly artificial faces or hands. Namibian people shown natural, dignified, contemporary.
- **Motion:** Slow, cinematic, restrained. Reduced-motion respected.
- **Reject:** Purple gradients, glassmorphism, excessive cards or pills, generic startup illustrations, random blobs, decorative dashboards, fake statistics, staged corporate stock photographs, mortarboard cliches, pyramids, classical symbols, generated text in images, oversized maroon blocks, em dashes, generic AI language.

## 8. Content principles

- Headlines: approximately 3-8 words.
- Supporting text: generally 20-60 words.
- Programme detail pages may contain more information.
- One clear message per section.
- Do not repeat the mission statement.
- Use meaningful captions to explain conceptual campus images.
- Every call to action leads somewhere real.
- Unknown information displays "To be confirmed", not invented content.
- Pre-launch content posture: no dates, no fees, no accreditation claims, no registration number, no campus address, no partnerships, no learner numbers.
- Status labels for planned programmes: "Planned", "Subject to approval", or "Register your interest".
- Prohibited words: world-class, revolutionary, cutting-edge, unlock, game-changing, seamless, unwavering commitment, next generation. Prohibited punctuation: em dashes.

## 9. Competitors and reference institutions

The brief directs research into current high-quality university, vocational, and African institutional websites for structural inspiration. The following are references for structure and craft, not for imitation:

- **Stanford University** for editorial composition, serif display typography, and disciplined grid.
- **African research institutions with strong editorial websites** for place-rooted identity.
- **Nordic vocational institutions** for restraint and material honesty.
- **Cultural institutions with magazine-style websites** for image-led narrative.

Oryx Institute does not compete with these institutions. They inform craft, not positioning.

Direct competitors (once Oryx Institute launches) will be Namibian vocational training providers, public training institutions, and private training academies. Competitive research will be conducted in Phase 2 (Content) when authorised facts are available.

- Not an e-commerce site. No online payments in pre-launch.
- Not a learning management system. No course content, no learner portal, no assessments.
- Not a staff intranet. No internal tools.
- Not a student records system. No learner data, no grades, no transcripts.
- Not a news outlet. Updates feed is for verified institutional updates only.
- Not a launch announcement. Pre-launch posture, no dates, no "opening soon" claims.
- Not a stock photography showcase. Original commissioned imagery only.
- Not a technology demo. No experimental frameworks, no beta features, no AI gimmicks.
- Not an accreditation claim. Status clearly stated as "subject to approval" or "to be confirmed" until verified facts are supplied.

## 12. Assumptions

Assumptions made in the absence of explicit user instruction. Each will be confirmed at the Phase 1 approval gate:

- The website is greenfield. No existing project stack to preserve.
- Next.js 16 with App Router is the appropriate stack: matches brief requirements for image optimisation (AVIF/WebP, responsive srcsets, lazy loading, priority loading), metadata API for canonical URLs and structured data, sitemap.ts and robots.ts, and Vercel deployment.
- Tailwind CSS v4 with brand tokens as CSS custom properties is the appropriate styling system: matches brief's "reusable editorial components without making every section look identical" without locking into a heavy design system.
- No component library (shadcn/ui or Astryx): the site is bespoke editorial marketing, not a component-heavy product UI. Astryx is explicitly not suitable per skill-stack.md ("Do not force it into a highly bespoke marketing site").
- No database in pre-launch: form submissions persisted to a local JSON file with audit log, clearly marked as mock behaviour, swappable to Vercel KV or Resend later without changing form code.
- No analytics in pre-launch unless user authorises a provider. Built with a clean analytics event interface so a provider can be added without code changes.
- No authentication in pre-launch.
- Founder page contains minimal copy: name, role, single short paragraph. No biography, no photograph of the founder unless supplied, no claims about credentials or experience.
- Programme pages display "To be confirmed" for every unknown field rather than invented content.
- Campus page clearly labels all imagery as architectural concept.
- Updates feed starts empty with a clear empty state explaining that verified updates will appear here.
- The Tangison Studio footer credit is present on every public page, linked to https://studio.tangison.com, accessible and visible.
