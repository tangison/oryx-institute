# CONTENT_PLAN.md - Oryx Institute

> The complete content plan and exact page copy for the Oryx Institute pre-launch website. Every word in this document is intended for production. No placeholders. No filler. No em dashes. No prohibited language. Copy is handed to `tangison-web-create` for implementation.

---

## 1. Objective

Produce exact, production-ready copy for every route, state, and interface element of the Oryx Institute pre-launch website. The copy must present a serious Namibian institution taking shape, using sparse, accurate, credible language. Every claim must be verified or marked "To be confirmed". Every call to action must lead to a real destination. The copy must avoid generic AI language, em dashes, and all prohibited terms listed in BRAND.md section 7.

## 2. Audience

(Per PRODUCT.md section 2.) Primary: prospective learners, employers, WIL partners, corporate training clients, research and advisory clients, funding and institutional partners, investors, the Namibian public. The copy addresses all audiences with the same restrained, credible, Namibian register. It does not adjust tone per segment.

## 3. Voice

Sparse. Accurate. Credible. Namibian. Active voice preferred. Short sentences for emphasis, longer sentences for explanation. Headlines are noun phrases or short statements. Calls to action are verbs. Captions explain what an image shows and why it matters. No puffery. No superlatives. No jargon. No em dashes.
