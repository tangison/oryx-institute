## Editorial rule

The Directory is learning material, not legal, tax, financial, insurance,
procurement, employment or accreditation advice. A legal or regulatory claim
ships only when the supporting source is named on the page and in this ledger.
A changing operational detail such as a fee, form, processing time, threshold,
rate or application portal is either checked directly with its responsible
authority at the point of use or left out.

The canonical links below are the Legal Assistance Centre's public
consolidated statute documents. Each identifies the underlying Government
Gazette source and amendments. The LAC is a public-interest law centre, not
the Gazette publisher, so its editorial annotations are not treated as
primary law. It is used here as a public, consolidated reading copy of the
Gazette text. The relevant Gazette identifier is recorded below.

## Research method and limits

1. Started with public statutory text and Government Gazette identifiers,
   rather than blogs, vendor pages or search-result snippets.
2. Accessed the public `LawCodex/na` mirror on GitHub on 13 November 2026 to
   inspect the LAC consolidated text, source URLs and section wording. The
   mirror declares its source collection was retrieved from LAC on 13 October
   2026. It is a research transport layer, **not** a published Directory
   source.
3. Used Scrapling with `--ai-targeted` for public GitHub document pages where
   extraction was needed. No authenticated material, personal data,
   paywalled content or disallowed crawl was used. No sizeable crawl was run.
4. Linked the canonical LAC document on each relevant Directory page so a
   reader can inspect the source. A direct current-authority check remains
   required before a reader files, hires, quotes tax, applies for funding or
   submits a bid.
5. Marked only section-level propositions as verified. Current operational
   requirements are deliberately not inferred from the statute alone.

## Recheck triggers

Reopen the affected page and ledger record when any of the following occurs:

- an Act, regulation, Gazette notice or consolidated statute changes;
- BIPA, NamRA, NQA, NTA, NAMFISA, the Ministry responsible for labour, the
  Social Security Commission or a public-procurement authority changes a form,
  rate, standard, registration process or published guidance;
- a reader reports an error with a source link or document version;
- a new Directory course needs a factual Namibia-specific claim.

No source record should be silently carried forward after its recheck date.
