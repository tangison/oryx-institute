## Page briefs

### / (home)

- **Purpose and primary conversion action:** Orient a new visitor, distinguish current work from future learning and make a factual office enquiry available.
- **Message:** A new polytechnic for Namibia with research, practice and operating ventures; it is working toward NTA training-provider registration and is not registered or accredited.
- **CTA:** Direct: enquire about current work. Transitional: understand current status or read the work.
- **Copy blocks:** Hero, institutional method, current work, research, ventures, Register and office close.

### /status

- **Purpose and primary conversion action:** State the current regulatory position plainly.
- **Message:** Status is dated and authoritative for the present disclosure; the Register is the history of confirmed facts.
- **CTA:** Direct: read the Register. Transitional: contact Oryx with a factual question.
- **Copy blocks:** Current position, non-claims, regulatory route, disclosed technical relationship and factual routes onward.

### /links and /contact

- **Purpose and primary conversion action:** Help a visitor select an approved public office route without uncertainty or copied links.
- **Message:** Official contacts and channels are collected in one place.
- **CTA:** Direct: use email, telephone, WhatsApp or the enquiry route. Transitional: open the relevant current-work or status page.
- **Copy blocks:** Direct contact, current work, institutional routes, official social channels and public particulars.

### /research, /practice and /ventures

- **Purpose and primary conversion action:** Let a visitor either inspect evidence, enquire about confirmed current work or understand a confirmed operating venture.
- **Message:** Each route describes only what Oryx can state now.
- **CTA:** Route-specific as listed in the Sitemap table.
- **Copy blocks:** Evidence or current-work explanation, supporting record and a factual next step.

## Lead capture

The public site does not use a lead magnet, account, quiz or advertising tracker. The supported handoff is an approved public contact route or the enquiry composer. Any future lead-capture mechanism requires a separate privacy, factual-status and user-value review before implementation.
