# Oryx Institute pre-launch market research

**Prepared:** 13 November 2026

**Purpose:** Establish a defensible pre-launch audience and channel strategy for Oryx Institute. This is a planning note, not an accreditation, employment, enrolment, partner, funding, or learner-outcome claim.

## Executive read

Oryx should lead with practical, public proof of work instead of promises. The strongest defensible audience is Namibian young adults who need a route into work, plus employers and partners who need a credible training and research counterpart. The site should make its pre-launch status, accreditation boundary, and contact route visible on every conversion path.

The channel mix should be website and WhatsApp first, with Facebook for broad local reach, Instagram for visual proof, and LinkedIn for employer and partner conversations. Do not open a channel until its owner, handle, response time, and approval path are confirmed.

## Demand signals

### Labour-market context

The Namibia Statistics Agency's 2023 Population and Housing Census labour-force presentation reports an official unemployment rate of 36.9%. It reports youth unemployment of 44.4% for people aged 15 to 34, a youth unemployment-plus-potential-labour-force rate of 61.4%, and a youth NEET rate of 41.9%. These figures support content that focuses on useful, work-facing skills, but they do not prove that an individual Oryx programme will improve employment outcomes.

**Source:** [Namibia Statistics Agency, 2023 Labour Force Report presentation, released 29 January 2025](https://nsa.org.na/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/2023-Labour-Force-Report-Presentation-for-the-Launch-Jan-2025-_OS.pdf)

### TVET relevance

The Namibia Training Authority's 2022 to 2026 Skills Development Plan says it is intended to improve access to quality TVET, improve relevance, and build stronger stakeholder partnerships. The NTA says its research uses Industry Skills Committees and that a scarce-skills validation survey involved more than 4,000 employers. This supports Oryx's research, practice, and employer-listening posture.

**Sources:**

- [NTA Skills Development Plan for the TVET Sector II, 2022 to 2026](https://www.nta.com.na/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Skills-Development-Plan-VET_2022-2026_1.pdf)
- [NTA research overview](https://www.nta.com.na/research/)

### Accreditation and funding boundary

The NTA's training-provider funding page says funding eligibility is tied to requirements including NTA registration and/or NQA accreditation, a qualification on the National Qualifications Framework, provider approval for the specific qualification and level, and demonstrated delivery capacity. Until Oryx has documentary evidence for an individual offer, site and social copy must not imply accredited qualifications, NTA-funded places, or recognised credentials.

**Source:** [NTA training-provider funding requirements](https://www.nta.com.na/training-provider-funding/)

## Channel evidence

DataReportal's January 2025 Namibia report estimates 754,000 social-media user identities, equal to 24.6% of the population and 42.8% of adults. It also estimates 754,000 Facebook users. The report warns that its social figures are not necessarily unique people and that platform methodology changes can affect comparisons over time.

**Source:** [DataReportal, Digital 2025: Namibia](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-namibia)

## Recommended audience model

| Audience | Need to address | Evidence Oryx can publish now | Primary action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Young adults considering skills development | A clear next step without false certainty | Short practical guides, transparent offer pages, pre-launch status, application contact | Write to the office or apply
| Micro-business owners and operators | A specific business problem, not generic motivation | Owner's Desk examples, templates, research notes, workshop briefs | Book a consultation conversation
| Employers and industry partners | A credible, responsive skills and research counterpart | Research process, scoped rapid-response offer, partner enquiry route | Propose a training or research need
| Future staff, instructors, and collaborators | A serious institution that does not invent appointments | Dated Register updates and open collaboration criteria | Contact the Principal's office

## Channel roles

| Channel | Job | Content that fits now | Avoid until verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website | Source of truth, search landing pages, application hand-off | Offer pages, Directory guides, research notes, Register updates, clear contact details | Accreditation badges, certificate imagery, campus claims
| WhatsApp and email | Human conversion and follow-up | Application acknowledgement, appointment coordination, approved FAQs | Unsecured collection of sensitive documents or unapproved automated replies
| Facebook | Local discovery and community distribution | Directory snippets, dated updates, practical checklists, event notices | Paid recruitment claims or unverified outcome claims
| Instagram | Quiet visual proof of the brand and work in progress | Brand still lifes, behind-the-scenes research process, concise Directory cards | Stock graduate imagery, fake classroom scenes, testimonial claims
| LinkedIn | Employer, partner, and practitioner credibility | Research methods, partnership invitations, transparent institutional updates | A company URL or staff title that has not been confirmed

## Pre-launch content pillars

1. **Working realities:** Namibia-specific guides, facts with sources, and questions gathered from operators
2. **Tools that hold:** Directory templates, short explainers, and practical process checklists
3. **Practice in public:** what the office can do now, how a brief is scoped, and what is not yet offered
4. **Research to action:** market-research methods, Bulletin notes, and evidence boundaries
5. **Building honestly:** dated Register entries, accreditation progress only when documented, and partner calls with named next steps

Each post should have one audience, one factual claim that can be sourced, one action, an owner, and a review date.

## Twelve-month operating rhythm

Start with capacity that can be sustained by one office.

- **Weekly:** one short practical post derived from a Directory guide or research note, reused for Facebook and Instagram
- **Fortnightly:** one website update, Bulletin note, or expanded Directory item with a source check
- **Monthly:** one Register update and one partner or employer-facing LinkedIn post after the organisation URL is confirmed
- **Quarterly:** review inquiries, application quality, source freshness, channel response time, and claims that need retirement or confirmation

Do not publish a calendar of promised programme launches, course dates, staff appointments, or accreditation milestones until the responsible evidence is available.

## Measurement plan

Track the following in a simple monthly sheet. Do not add analytics or cookies without updating the privacy notice and obtaining any required approvals.

| Measure | What it shows | Decision it supports |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified email or WhatsApp enquiries | Whether content brings the intended audience | Which topics and channels deserve another month
| Offer-page to application hand-off rate | Whether a page gives a clear next step | Which pages need clearer eligibility, price, date, or contact information
| Directory downloads or page reads | Whether practical material is useful | Which templates and guides to update or expand
| Partner conversations with a named need | Whether employer-facing material earns trust | Which sectors to research before building an offer
| Median first-response time | Whether the office can meet the promise of a human response | Whether to reduce posting frequency or add operating support
| Source-review completion | Whether public claims remain current | Which pages must be corrected, dated, or unpublished

## Evidence and publication gates

Before publishing any certificate, course date, staff profile, partner logo, social profile, music, or launch announcement, attach the source record and named approver.

| Asset or claim | Required before publication |
|---|---|
| BIPA registration number or certificate | Redacted copy plus publication approval
| NQA or NTA status | Exact document, status wording, valid date, and owner approval
| Course fees, dates, instructors, or delivery site | Approved offer sheet with a review date
| Learner, client, or partner story | Written permission, factual review, and image consent
| Music or sound | Licence, territory, duration, attribution requirement, format, and accessibility alternative
| Social profile | Exact URL, account owner, recovery contact, response standard, and publishing authority

## Open decisions

1. The supplied brand note says the `理工` mark belongs beside the wordmark, while the prior delivery direction removed Chinese public content. Decide whether `理工` is an approved visual brand mark only, an approved textual label, or remains retired.
2. Confirm official Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp URLs before the site links to them. The LinkedIn company slug in the repository is not confirmed.
3. Supply redacted BIPA and accreditation material before the Register or metadata names a status beyond the current pre-launch wording.
4. Supply music licences and preferred placement before audio is added. Audio must have controls, captions or transcript where speech is used, and a reduced-motion or silent experience.
5. Confirm whether the new Vercel project should keep its generated `.vercel.app` address only until the institute domain and certificate are ready.

## Research limits

This note uses official Namibia Statistics Agency and Namibia Training Authority material where available, plus DataReportal for platform-planning context. The social-media figures are advertising and platform estimates, not a count of unique people. This note does not claim that the sources reviewed prove demand for an individual Oryx offer.
