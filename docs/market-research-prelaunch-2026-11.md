# Oryx prelaunch publishing guardrails

**Status note:** Oryx is working toward NTA training-provider registration. It is not registered or accredited. This document is an editorial guardrail, not an institutional-status source; the Institutional Status page is authoritative for the current public disclosure.

## Purpose

Public communications should make the useful work available now easy to understand without creating a claim about future learning, registration, accreditation or public premises.

## Approved public subjects

| Surface | Suitable content | Do not imply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Website | Research Notes, current business counselling and market research, dated Register updates, factual institutional status | Accredited programmes, NQF credits, open campuses, intake or fees |
| Instagram and Facebook | Brand still lifes, research process, a short sourced observation, current-work context, confirmed public updates | Stock graduate imagery, classroom claims, testimonials, certificate imagery or recruitment claims |
| LinkedIn and X | Links to research, current work and confirmed institutional updates | Regulatory endorsement, provider registration, accreditation or outcomes not evidenced |
| Link Hub | Official contact routes, official channels, research, current work, Register and Status | A Business Profile that is not verified, discontinued Directory links or third-party profile pages |

## Status wording

When public copy needs to mention institutional status, use the approved substance:

> Oryx is working toward NTA training-provider registration. It is not registered or accredited.

NQA accreditation is a future process. It must not be described as in progress, guaranteed, jointly pursued with the NTA or implied through a mark, link placement or visual treatment.

## Publishing checks

Before publishing a post, page or graphic:

1. Confirm the claimed fact has a source record and an accountable approver.
2. Confirm the copy does not revive the discontinued Oryx Directory or promote its former resources and templates.
3. Confirm current work is distinguished from future learning pathways.
4. Confirm contact details and social links match `src/lib/site.ts`.
5. Link to the Institutional Status page whenever a reader may reasonably ask about regulatory status.

## Evidence and review

No traffic, ranking, conversion, enrolment or demand claim should be made until measured data is available. A post is not made stronger by frequency alone. If its evidence is not ready, it remains unpublished.
