## What this research does not establish

Do **not** infer or publish any of the following as fact without a direct, current confirmation from the appropriate owner, operator or authority:

- current operation, availability, prices, booking process, rooms, amenities, imagery, accessibility or visitor policy
- operator identity, ownership, lease, takeover, property control, site approval or an Oryx legal relationship to the property
- coordinates, exact map pin, directions, public visits or an opening date
- a guesthouse-specific email address; the only email shown by the source is the Namibia Tourism registry address
- Oryx programmes, qualifications, NQF credits, fees, intakes, accreditation or course dates

## Publication decision on 13 November 2026

Use the directory-recorded locality, road and telephone only with clear Visit Namibia attribution and the Oryx/non-booking boundary. Link to the Visit Namibia directory record and use a textual Google Maps search query for the recorded location rather than the unavailable location-sharing URL.

A fuller guesthouse profile still requires a direct, current confirmation of the property’s operator, public-access and booking details, and written approval of any image or amenity claims.
