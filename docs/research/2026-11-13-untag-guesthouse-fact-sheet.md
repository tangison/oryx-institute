## Location wording

Use the map as a **location reference**, not as evidence of an open campus, property control, public visiting route or guesthouse booking address.

Approved short form:

> Untag Guesthouse is a planned future hospitality-focused site identified by Oryx. It is not open for Oryx courses or enrolment.

Approved expanded form:

> Oryx identifies Untag Guesthouse as a planned future hospitality-focused site. The site is not open. Hospitality, tour guiding, safety and administration are possible future areas only. Oryx does not currently offer courses, enrolment, qualifications or accredited programmes there.

## Not established for publication

Do not publish or infer any of the following from the approved record or the map reference:

- a street address, coordinates, village, road, directions or public visiting instructions
- ownership, lease, takeover, possession, approval, construction or opening status
- a guesthouse operator, booking route, telephone number, email address, price, availability, room count or amenities
- programme names, course dates, qualifications, NQF credits, fees, intakes or accreditation
- an opening date, partner relationship or local-regulator endorsement

## Facts needed before a fuller public profile

A future public profile needs written approval for each field. At minimum, confirm the exact public address or location wording, public visiting policy, operator and booking-contact approval, Oryx's legal relationship to the site, opening status, imagery rights, accessible-route information and a current date for the record.

Until those facts are approved, the map reference and planned-site disclosure are the complete public record.
