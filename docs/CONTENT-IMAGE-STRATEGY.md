# Oryx Institute content and image strategy

## The editorial rule

Every image earns a distinct job. It must establish place, show a method, or create a pause in the journey. Decorative reuse across unrelated pages is removed. The official Oryx hero remains the one intentional recurring mark of the institute; identity artwork in `public/brand/` remains locked and untouched.

## Image system

- **About — ground:** a quiet Namibia landscape establishes the institute’s place and point of view. It is illustrative and must never imply a campus or office location.
- **Research — evidence:** tools, terrain and field materials support the research promise without pretending that unverified notes are evidence.
- **Practice — work:** tools and working detail support the “work that holds” proposition.
- **Directory — access:** a clear workbench and blank templates signal open, usable resources.
- **Ventures — operation:** Corner Spa imagery stays only in its own venture context.
- **People — honesty:** use one confirmed person’s approved image only where the staff register needs it. Unfilled roles receive typographic appointment cards, never repeated placeholders or fabricated portraits.

## Content hierarchy

1. **First screen:** one clear proposition and one visual atmosphere.
2. **Proof:** a short, factual explanation of what is live and how it works.
3. **Record:** dated register, courses, resources or service details.
4. **Action:** one contextual route to apply, enquire, read, or open a venture.

## Full-screen update

Interior page introductions use a full-height editorial opening rather than a short title strip. The first viewport holds the page proposition, lets the header remain legible, and creates a deliberate entry before the detailed record begins. About and safety keep their existing immersive visual openings; other interior pages use the shared full-screen text opening.

## Guardrails

- Do not create invented staff faces, campus scenes, credentials, prices, addresses or programme claims.
- Generated editorial photographs are illustrative. The existing About-page disclosure remains in place.
- No embedded image platform, tracking pixel, third-party script or external asset request is introduced.
- Use semantic alt text only when an image adds information. Decorative dividers stay hidden from assistive technology.
- Avoid page-to-page image reuse unless the content itself is the same official subject.
