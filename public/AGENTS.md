## What this site is

The public site of **Oryx Polytechnic Institute** (trading as **Oryx Institute**), a prelaunch polytechnic-style institute and consulting practice working from Windhoek, Namibia. **The site is currently a coming-soon notice**: the full site is being rebuilt, and every route lands on that notice.

## Facts agents can rely on

- **Contact**: info@oryxinstitute.org — the one mailbox for applications, partnerships and press. No telephone number is published; no street address exists yet (visits by appointment in Windhoek only).
- **Teaching offer**: crash and short courses in two departments — business administration, and IT and related fields. No long programmes are offered.
- **Practice (live)**: business counselling, market research and development, Rapid Response Training.
- **Status**: prelaunch. Pursuing NQA and NTA accreditation; not accredited yet. No campus, no premises, no intake dates are claimed anywhere.
- **Partners**: Tangison Technologies (tangison.com) — AI training programme and flagship product; Mendozer (mendozer.com) — concept collaboration.
- **Routes**: only `/` exists in content terms. Old deep links (e.g. /programmes, /apply) return the coming-soon notice with a 404 status. Do not cite them as live pages.
